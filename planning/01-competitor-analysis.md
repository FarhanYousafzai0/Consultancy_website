## Details

### AbroadDreaming
- **Good:** very wide toolset — SOP/LOM reviewer, mock interview voice AI, visa tracker, document vault, cost calculator. Best idea: **Study Agents** — a bot watches a specific official page and alerts when a deadline, fee or requirement changes (Free: 1 agent weekly, Premium: 5 agents daily).
- **Weak:**
  - 50+ countries, so Germany gets no depth.
  - AI-generated data with errors (seen in screenshots: past deadlines, "€0" TUM tuition, non-German universities in results, a 93% match on a German-taught program for a student with no German).
  - Core matching is paywalled (6 matches, 3 scholarships, 1 analysis on free plan).
- **Learn:** Study Agents concept; wide document toolkit.

### MyGermanUniversity (biggest Germany-specific player)
- **Good:**
  - Lists every program they can find (more than DAAD or Hochschulkompass).
  - Unique filters: uni-assist, IELTS bands, TOEFL, Cambridge, deadlines, rankings.
  - ~150 free webinars per year, plus a free self-study academy.
- **Weak:**
  - A search engine, not an advisor: no eligibility check, no application help.
  - Ranking is influenced by paid university profiles (disclosed).
- **Business model:** students pay nothing; universities pay for premium profiles; affiliate fees from blocked accounts and insurance; lead fees from info-pack requests.
- **Learn:** filter set; webinar-driven growth; university-side revenue.

### Deutics
- **Good:**
  - Positioning: "programs you can actually get into", ranked against published requirements.
  - No account needed; data stays in the browser; clear affiliate disclosure.
- **Weak:** no application help, scholarships, or post-admission support.
- **Learn:** honest positioning; zero-friction first use.

### UniAdvisorAI
- **Good:**
  - Practical Germany tools: German CV builder (DIN 5008), grade calculator (modified Bavarian formula), cost-of-living by city.
  - Kanban application tracker; SOP generator.
- **Weak:** AI matching with no country-specific eligibility rules; no human support.

### German Path
- **Good:**
  - 20,000+ programs, plain-English AI search.
  - **One-time credits instead of a subscription** — friendly for price-sensitive students.
  - Guides written per country.
- **Weak:** documents and search only; no eligibility logic, no consultant.
- **Learn:** credit-based pricing for AI document tools.

### AbroBot (closest to our "consultancy + AI" model)
- **Good:**
  - Free self-serve app plus "Concierge": a named human counsellor runs the whole case, priced per case after a call.
  - "Checked [date]" on data; claims no commission bias.
  - Realistic advice ("public universities will be tight; a HAW in Berlin is the realistic route").
- **Weak:** India-focused; Germany is one of many countries.
- **Learn:** free app + paid human service on the same platform.

### Applywise (Pakistan competitor)
- **Good:** honest about the public vs. private trade-off; covers APS, Studienkolleg, blocked account.
- **Weak:** only pushes private universities (GISMA, IU, Arden, UE, BSBI) because they pay commission.
- **Learn:** confirms Pakistani students need APS; confirms many students lack a public-university route without Studienkolleg.

### Traditional agencies (Sarem, The Edvisors)
- **Good:** full hand-holding — APS, uni-assist, visa appointment, mock interview, post-arrival help; fast WhatsApp contact.
- **Weak:** expensive, quality depends on the counsellor, nothing transparent for the student.

## Patterns across the market
1. **Data:** serious players build their own database from university websites and show when it was checked. Weak players let AI generate facts.
2. **Freshness:** "last checked" dates and change-watching bots are becoming expected.
3. **Money:** Germany-only sites earn from universities and affiliates. Consultancies earn from human services or private-university commissions. Few succeed with student subscriptions alone.
4. **Human help:** the strongest model is free software + paid human service using the same AI.
