# User Journey

## Main journey
1. **Arrive** — from a search or social post (e.g. "Can I study in Germany with FSc?", "English-taught CS Master's in Germany").
2. **Free eligibility check, no sign-up** — 60 seconds: country, qualification, target level, field, English score. Instant answer: eligible directly / need Studienkolleg / need APS / realistic options.
3. **Create account** — the check becomes a light profile. Live matches appear immediately.
4. **Complete profile** — each new field visibly unlocks more results ("Add your GPA → 12 more programs ranked").
5. **Browse & filter** — "Show all" or "Only what I qualify for".
6. **Shortlist & compare** — 3–5 programs: cost, deadline, language, route, fit.
7. **Funding** — matched scholarships with honest odds; cost plan (tuition, semester fee, blocked account).
8. **Prepare** — per-program document checklist, grade converter, document tools (later), timeline.
9. **Apply** — guided steps for uni-assist / direct / VPD, tracker (later).
10. **Admitted** — APS, blocked account, health insurance, visa appointment, housing (later).
11. **Arrive in Germany** — Anmeldung, residence permit, bank, SIM (later).
12. **Report outcome** — admitted / rejected → improves future matching (later).

**At every step:** AI Advisor (later) and "Talk to a consultant" button (MVP).

## Key moments that build trust
- First answer is honest, even if it's "you need Studienkolleg first".
- Every fact shows source + last verified date.
- Matching is free and unlimited — no paywall before value.
