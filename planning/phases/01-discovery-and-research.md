## Open questions for grill-me
1. Who is our **first** target student: FSc/HSSC → Bachelor's, BS → Master's, Ausbildung, or PhD?
2. Which countries first: Pakistan only, or Pakistan + India + Bangladesh + Middle East?
3. How many current consultancy clients do we have, and what do they pay for today?
4. What are the 3 most common reasons our clients get rejected or give up?
5. Who will run the student interviews, and how do we recruit interviewees?
6. Are we a consultancy that uses software, or a software product that offers consultants? (Decides everything else.)

## Answers
- **Q6 — Consultancy or software?** A **consultancy that uses free software** to attract students. Revenue comes mainly from consultant services; the platform is the free, useful front door. *(2026-10-04)*
- **Q1 — First target student?** All three from launch: **BS/BSc → Master's**, **FSc/HSSC → Bachelor's** (incl. Studienkolleg / private universities), and **Ausbildung**. *(2026-10-04)*
  - **Depth at launch:** Master's = **full** (verified programs, matching, scholarships, checklists). Bachelor's = **medium** (eligibility check: direct / Studienkolleg / foundation year; curated list of Studienkollegs + English-taught Bachelor's, public and private; consultant handoff). Ausbildung = **medium** (eligibility check: German level + certificate; live listings via Bundesagentur für Arbeit API; consultant handoff).
- **Q2 — Which countries first?** **Pakistan only**, fully built (FSc/HSSC, A-levels, 4-year BS vs. 2-year BA/BSc, APS). System designed so more countries can be added later as extra rule sets. *(2026-10-04)*
- **Q3a — What do clients pay for today?** University shortlisting / counselling, full application handling (uni-assist / direct), SOP / motivation letter / CV writing, scholarship applications. **Not offered today:** APS help, visa file / interview prep, language classes, Ausbildung placement, private-university commissions. *(2026-10-04)*
  - **Ausbildung follow-up:** free self-serve only at launch (eligibility check, live listings, guides) — **no consultant handoff**. Track Ausbildung usage; add a paid service or partner referral later if demand is strong. APS and visa = free guides only. *(2026-10-04)*
- **Q3b — Client volume?** **Fewer than 20 paying clients per year** (early-stage business). The platform's main job is to **grow** the consultancy, not make an existing team more efficient. Interviews must be recruited beyond our own clients. *(2026-10-04)*
- **Q5 — Who runs interviews and how?** The **founder** runs 30-minute calls. Target: 5 FSc/HSSC → Bachelor's, 5 BS → Master's, 3–5 Pakistani students already in Germany. Recruit via Pakistani "Study in Germany" Facebook/WhatsApp groups, LinkedIn, existing clients, university societies. Thank-you: free 20-minute counselling session (also generates first leads). *(2026-10-04)*
- **Q4 — Why do students get rejected or give up?** (founder's experience; interviews will rank them) — and the feature that answers each:

  | Pain point | Platform answer |
  |---|---|
  | Not eligible (Studienkolleg, 2-year degree, wrong subject) | Free eligibility check (Pakistan rules) |
  | IELTS too low / no German | Language filters + "only what I qualify for" |
  | Can't afford blocked account / living costs | Cost plan + scholarships with honest odds |
  | Missed deadlines / started too late | Verified deadlines + email alerts + timeline |
  | Applied to wrong / too competitive programs | Rules-based matching with Reach / Match / Safety |
  | Weak or incomplete documents | Per-program checklist → paid document service (consultant) |

  *(2026-10-04)*
