## Open questions for grill-me
1. What is the **one** thing the MVP must do better than MyGermanUniversity and Deutics?
2. Is the AI chat in the MVP (even limited) or after?
3. Is Ausbildung in the MVP?
4. Do private universities appear in matching at launch, and how are they labeled?
5. Which UI languages at launch (English, Urdu)?
6. What number of verified programs is "enough" for launch?
7. What is the launch success target after 3 months (users, consultations, revenue)?

## Answers
- **Q1 — The one thing the MVP does better than anyone?** *"In 60 seconds, a Pakistani student learns honestly whether they can study in Germany and which programs they can actually get into — with verified data and a real consultant one click away."* This is the product promise and the main marketing message. *(2026-10-04)*
- **Q2 — AI chat in MVP?** **Yes, limited.** It answers only from our own guides and the top 20 student questions (from Phase 1 interviews). Anything outside that → "I don't have verified information" + WhatsApp consultant handoff. Full tool-based advisor (searching programs, checking eligibility) stays in build phase 3. *(2026-10-04)*
- **Q4 — Private universities?** **Public and private both shown**, clearly labeled with total cost. Ranked by fit and cost, **never by payment**; public options shown first when the student qualifies. Any future commission must be disclosed. *(2026-10-04)*
- **Q5 — UI languages?** **English only everywhere** at launch (website, AI chat, content). Urdu can be revisited later based on demand. *(2026-10-04)*
- **Q6 — Verified data at launch?** **150–200 Master's programs** (CS/IT, data/AI, electrical & mechanical engineering, business/management) + **all public Studienkollegs** + **30–50 English-taught Bachelor's** (public and private). Estimated ~50–65 hours of human checking (confirm with Phase 4 pilot). New fields added monthly after launch. *(2026-10-04)*
- **Q3 — Ausbildung in MVP?** Already answered in Phase 1: yes, medium depth, free self-serve, no consultant handoff.
- **Q7 — 3-month success targets?** 1,000 free eligibility checks → 300 accounts → 100 consultant handoffs (WhatsApp/chat) → 30 consultations booked → **5–10 new paying clients**. Main metric = new paying clients. Adjust after first month of real traffic. *(2026-10-04)*
