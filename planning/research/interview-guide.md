# Student Interview Guide (Phase 1)

**Length:** 30 minutes · **Run by:** founder · **Thank-you:** free 20-minute counselling session

## Who to interview
| Group | Target | Done |
|---|---|---|
| FSc/HSSC → Bachelor's | 5 | 0 |
| BS → Master's | 5 | 0 |
| Pakistani students already in Germany | 3–5 | 0 |

**Where to find them:** Pakistani "Study in Germany" Facebook groups and WhatsApp communities, LinkedIn (Pakistani students at German universities), existing clients, university societies in Pakistan.

## Rules
- Ask about what they **did**, not what they **would** do.
- Don't pitch the platform until the last 5 minutes.
- Ask "why?" and "what happened next?" often.
- Write down their exact words for pain points.

## Questions

### Background (3 min)
1. What are you studying / what did you study, and what's your grade?
2. Which degree do you want in Germany, and in which field?
3. What's your English level? Any German?

### Their journey so far (10 min)
4. Walk me through everything you've done so far to study in Germany, step by step.
5. Where did you look for information? (Websites, groups, YouTube, agents, friends)
6. What was the most confusing part?
7. What took much longer than you expected?
8. Did you ever find out something too late? What?

### Eligibility, language, money (7 min)
9. How did you find out whether you were eligible? Were you sure?
10. How did you choose which universities to apply to?
11. How are you planning to pay (blocked account, living costs)? Did you look for scholarships — what happened?
12. Did you miss a deadline or almost miss one? Why?

### Help and money (5 min)
13. Have you used a consultant or agent? What did you pay, and was it worth it?
14. What would you happily pay for? What should always be free?
15. If you could fix one thing about this whole process, what would it be?

### Close (5 min)
16. (Optional) Show the idea in one sentence — what's your first reaction?
17. Can we contact you again when we have something to test?
18. Book their free counselling session.
